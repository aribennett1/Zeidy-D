## Shabbos — The Gift of Olam Haba in This World

### PDF page 477 — printed page 451
- **Search:** `this is a קשיא which was bothered by the greats of all time`
- **Issue:** Clear grammatical error. A question can **bother** people; it is not normally **“bothered by”** them.
- **Suggested correction:** `this is a קשיא which bothered the greats of all time`

### PDF page 477 — printed page 451
- **Search:** `also was bothered what is this new מתנה is giving כלל ישראל`
- **Issue:** The clause is grammatically broken: it is missing the subject of **“is giving,”** and the construction `was bothered what is...` is incomplete.
- **Suggested correction:** Something along the lines of: `was also bothered by the question of what this new מתנה is that Hashem is giving כלל ישראל`
- **Note:** This one does require a tiny bit more intervention than the other corrections, but the existing sentence is sufficiently broken that I would fix it in a final pass.

- Transliteration choices, capitalization conventions, or wording that is merely awkward rather than wrong.
- Source requests or places where a source could be added.
- Hebrew/Yiddish phrases merely because text extraction displayed them in an odd order; I only wanted to flag those if the rendered page itself was clearly wrong.
